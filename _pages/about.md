I am a recent graduate in Computer Science and Engineering (Major: Information Systems) from the American International University–Bangladesh (AIUB), where I completed my B.Sc. with a CGPA of 3.89/4.0. My research focuses on machine learning and explainable AI for climate extremes and heat-health risk, with an emphasis on combining ground-station time series with remote sensing and spatial data for spatiotemporal prediction. I work with deep learning architectures such as GRU and LSTM, gradient-boosted models like XGBoost, and explainability methods including SHAP and LIME.

My first-author paper, *"Enhancing Heatwave Prediction Using Explainable AI: Feature Selection and Model Optimization for Improved Forecasting,"* was accepted at COMSYS 2025, presented at the conference, and is to appear in the proceedings (Springer Nature). I worked as a Research Assistant under Nuzhat Tabassum (Assistant Professor, AIUB) from May 2025 to March 2026, and we continue to collaborate on a manuscript on forecasting next-day maximum temperature and hot days at Bangladeshi weather stations. For this, I built and quality-controlled a 44-year (1981–2024) daily dataset from 35 Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) stations and benchmarked 22 machine-learning, time-series and deep-learning models against persistence, climatology and ridge regression under a strict leakage-free setup. A leave-one-station-out test on 32 stations showed a pooled XGBoost model beating persistence at every held-out station.

I am currently a Research Student at Elite Research Lab, where I receive structured mentorship in manuscript writing and work in mentor-led research groups, currently co-authoring a team paper. In parallel, I am independently developing a machine learning framework for next-day heat risk prediction (Safe, Caution, Danger, Extreme Danger) across eight cities in Bangladesh, using Heat Index–derived risk labels and SHAP to identify city-specific risk factors toward an early-warning framework.

I am applying to PhD programs for 2027 entry. I want to work on climate-resilient AI: combining weather-station records with satellite data to predict heat risk, including in places that have no weather station, with models that are interpretable enough to inform real-world early-warning systems in climate-vulnerable regions like South Asia.
