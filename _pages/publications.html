Forecasting Next-Day Maximum Temperature and Hot Days at Bangladeshi Weather Stations: A Leakage-Free Comparison of Machine-Learning, Time-Series and Deep-Learning Models against Persistence

With N. Tabassum (AIUB). In preparation.

A quality-controlled 44-year (1981–2024) daily dataset from 35 BMD stations; 22 machine-learning, time-series and deep-learning models benchmarked against persistence, climatology and ridge regression under a strict leakage-free next-day setup, plus a leave-one-station-out test on 32 stations.
