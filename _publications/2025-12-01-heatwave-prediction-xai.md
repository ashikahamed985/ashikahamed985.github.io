This paper addresses heatwave prediction in Dhaka, Bangladesh using a 10-year 
(2010–2020) climate dataset. We applied SHAP-based feature selection to identify 
the key predictors of daily maximum temperature, then implemented and benchmarked 
four architectures — GRU, LSTM, RNN, and XGBoost — to forecast heatwave events. 
Model performance was evaluated using MSE, MAE, and R² metrics, with the aim of 
improving both the accuracy and interpretability of climate extreme forecasting 
for public early-warning applications.
